Judging criteria & deliverables

SIXT Hackathon 2026

What to hand in (by 16:45, in the GitHub repo)

  • Code in your project folder (in your clone or fork), and enter the link to your GitHub repo in the form (public, or private with your topic owner added as a collaborator by email or GitHub username). The repo link is your submission.
  • SUBMISSION.md in your project folder: approach, results, how to run.
  • Either a 1-minute demo video (submission/demo.mp4, under 50 MB) or a small set of slides (submission/slides.pdf, max 5 slides), included in the same repo. Your choice. It counts as a deliverable alongside the code.
  • Evaluation results, with a baseline for comparison if one exists.
  • Whatever extra your project README asks for. No real data, no secrets.

How you are judged (100 points)

CriterionPointsWhat we look for
Problem understanding15You grasp the business problem and its constraints
Quality of approach25Sound, justified method; compared against a baseline if one exists
Results25Measured evidence for your approach, honestly evaluated
Business relevance & reusability15Could SIXT use it? Clear next steps
Code & reproducibility10Runs from SUBMISSION.md, readable, clean
Presentation & demo10Clear story, working demo, good answers

Up to +5 bonus for an exceptional insight or particularly clean engineering.

How the evening works

  1. 16:45 – 17:45 Reviews and finalist selection: your topic owner looks at your submission (code, video or slides) and may ask a clarifying question. If your owner is not on site, they review it remotely from your GitHub link. There is no formal team pitch during the reviews, so please make your submission clear. The topic owners then select 6 finalist teams across all topics. If a topic has no submission that meets the owner's expectations, the next best submission from another topic takes the place.
  2. 17:45 – 18:20 Final pitches (the finalists are announced just before) by the 6 finalist teams, 3 min pitch + 2 min Q&A each, scored by the jury on the same criteria.
